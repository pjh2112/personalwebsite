# Academic
---

My academic work focuses on the ethical dimensions of political systems, specifically regarding international relations and development initiatives. I hope to better understand how the lives of people around the world can be improved through greater economic opportunity and political freedom. From a philosophical standpoint, these perspectives are informed by modern and contemporary conceptions of natural rights, justice, and freedom. By the time I finish my undergraduate degree, I hope to have built up a solid base of applicable research and writing that will help me progress towards my goals of improving equity and opportunity around the world.
