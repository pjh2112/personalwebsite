At the Ballard Center, I am a member of the continuous improvement team and serve as the case study specialist. In my role, I author case studies used for both internal and external publishing, with the intention of showcasing the Center's projects and successes in written format. 

The following are accomplishments made in my role since starting in August 2025.

- Prototyped and developed a decision making model for the Ballard Center, complete with a decision matrix, fillable form, examples, and flowchart 
- Standardized a new model for mentorship within the Ballard Center. Assisted in editing and drafting a 20 page manual and accompanying training to promote to other universities in the social impact sphere.
- Stepped into a new role as Case Study Specialist, developing a strategy to help the Ballard Center examine real applications of the social impact cycle both inside and outside the center. This role functions to create case studies showing the successes and shortcomings of various endeavors with the goals of training employees, keeping an accurate history, and showcasing the power of the social impact cycle at work.
